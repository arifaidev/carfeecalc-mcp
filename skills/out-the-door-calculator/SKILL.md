# Out-the-Door Calculator

Build an itemized vehicle purchase estimate.

Calculate from purchase price, taxable discounts/trade-in treatment, state/local sales or use tax, title, registration, plate, EV surcharges, and other verified government charges. Dealer documentation fees and add-ons must be explicitly identified rather than silently mixed into government fees.

Always label estimates, assumptions, and exclusions. Verify the applicable jurisdiction before producing a final number.
